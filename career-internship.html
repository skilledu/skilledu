Internship & Certification Questions

SkillEdU provides career-oriented technical training that prepares students for internship roles. After completing course modules and required capstone projects, eligible learners can apply to available internship opportunities posted through our network and official LinkedIn job board. Hiring decisions, scopes of work, and day-to-day supervision are managed directly by the hiring companies.

Internship completion certificates are issued directly by the hiring companies where students complete their internship tenure, not by SkillEdU. SkillEdU awards a course completion certificate acknowledging the technical training and academic capstone projects completed during your program.
Eligibility & Selection Questions

Eligibility generally requires successful completion of your SkillEdU training program, submission of all required capstone projects with public GitHub repositories, and passing an internal code review to ensure your work meets baseline development standards.

The process begins with an internal readiness check by SkillEdU mentors to verify project quality and code readiness. Once cleared, candidates apply to company openings. Participating companies conduct their own independent evaluation, which may include coding tests, technical interviews, and discussions before selecting candidates.
Career Support & Application Process

There is no on-site application form, registration portal, or login system on this website. All open internship listings and partner opportunities are published on the official SkillEdU LinkedIn Job Board. Candidate applications are submitted directly through the external employer's application link or designated hiring channel.

SkillEdU supports students through resume reviews for applicant tracking system (ATS) readability, GitHub profile cleanup, portfolio presentation guidance, and mock interview sessions covering core technical concepts, coding exercises, and general communication.
