Fee & Non-Refund Terms Applies to: All Modules & Programs

This Refund Policy outlines Skilled.U's official terms regarding tuition, module fees, and training payments. Prospective students and parents are requested to review these terms carefully before completing any enrollment or fee payment.

1 Strict No-Refund Rule

All Fees Are Strictly Non-Refundable

Once a fee payment is made toward any Skilled.U course, module, or workshop, no refund is issued regardless of how many days have passed since payment or enrollment.

Skilled.U maintains a clear, zero-exception no-refund policy across every training track. There is no grace period or cooling-off window after a transaction is completed.

2 Why We Follow a Module-Based Fee Model

Rather than requiring heavy upfront lump-sum commitments, Skilled.U designs its fee collection to be practical, accessible, and module-based. Learners pay in manageable stages aligned with individual training modules. Because fees are already structured affordably on a per-module basis, resources, mentor allocation, and seat reservations are committed immediately upon payment, making every settled fee final.

3 No Full or Partial Refunds

Skilled.U does not grant either full fee reversals or partial/prorated refunds. Discontinuing a batch mid-way, missing scheduled classes, changing personal availability, or deciding not to continue after registration does not qualify for any full or partial refund.

4 No Conditional Exceptions

There are no qualifying conditions, special waivers, or exceptional scenarios under which a refund is approved. Every enrollment and module payment is accepted on the clear understanding that fees are non-refundable under all circumstances.

5 No Automated or Manual Refund Process

Because refunds are never issued, Skilled.U does not operate any refund processing system—there is neither an automated online refund mechanism nor a manual refund request or disbursement workflow at the institute. Requests submitted for fee reversals cannot be processed.

6 Pre-Enrollment Guidance

We encourage every student to review curriculum details, batch timings, and module fee breakdowns thoroughly before paying. If you need clarification prior to enrollment, please speak with our admissions team at +91 89890 99962 or email info@skilledu.in. You can also review our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy for complete program terms.
